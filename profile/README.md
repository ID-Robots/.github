## What we build

### 🛩️ SkyHub — autonomous drone fleet operations

A cloud platform for planning, flying and monitoring drone and rover fleets built on
**ArduPilot** and **PX4**. Mission planning, live telemetry, video streaming and fleet
management for persistent autonomous operations — one control plane for many vehicles.

→ [skyhub.ai](https://skyhub.ai) · [docs.skyhub.ai](https://docs.skyhub.ai)

### 🦞 ClawBox — a private AI assistant you own

A dedicated always-on AI appliance built on **NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano** (67 TOPS, 512 GB NVMe).
It runs [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) 24/7 with local models, browser
automation and messaging integrations. One-time purchase, your data stays on the device,
cloud AI strictly opt-in.

→ [clawbox.com](https://clawbox.com) · [docs.clawbox.com](https://docs.clawbox.com)
